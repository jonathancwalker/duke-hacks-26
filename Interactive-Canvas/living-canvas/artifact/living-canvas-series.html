Living Canvas Series

Monet painted Rouen Cathedral more than thirty times, and only the light changed. Here, the crowd is the light. Each person's words, voice and movement are encoded into the painting's latent space, added to everyone before them, and the whole canvas is repainted from that new state. The math

e = Ewords(text) + 0.6·Evoice(tone) + 0.6·Emove(effort)
novelty = ‖e − C‖ (how different you are)
C ← C + α(e − C) α grows with novelty
painting ← repaint(painting, C, strength s)
Encode. Claude reads your words and places them on 8 axes. Tone and movement add their own pull.
Add. Your vector pulls the crowd vector toward you. Surprising expressions pull harder.
Decode. A share of the canvas is repainted from the new state, on top of the last painting, so every visitor stays in the layers.

What would make it theirs

Stand-ins in this demo: Claude encodes words into 8 named painting dimensions, tone and movement come from the pickers, and a brush-stroke renderer decodes the vector into the painting. The booth version records voice through a mic and movement through a webcam, encodes them into a shared embedding space (CLIP or ImageBind), and decodes with a diffusion model in image-to-image mode.
