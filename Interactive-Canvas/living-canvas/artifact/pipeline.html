Living Canvas Pipeline

A visitor speaks and moves for ten seconds. We read how they were, place that in a space built from eighteen real paintings, and the chapel over the pond is repainted in the manner the crowd has become. Each person gets a moment of their own, then settles into everyone before them.

The pipeline

Five stations, one visit in about 6 seconds

1 · CaptureBooth laptop · Chrome

Read the person

  • Webcam → MediaPipe Pose tracks both wrists → Laban effort: weight, time, space, flow
  • Mic → loudness, pitch movement, syllable rate → vocal arousal
  • Chrome speech recognition → words
10 s window
2 · Art directorGemini 3.8 Flash · Vertex AI

Decide how it's painted

  • Words give meaning and feeling; body and voice set energy
  • Returns structured JSON: energy, warmth, light in −1…1, plus a museum-label reading
  • Never changes the subject, only the manner
3–6 s
3 · Art spaceCLIP ViT-H image space

Place them among real paintings

  • 18 public-domain works, Rembrandt to Kandinsky, embedded by the IP-Adapter's own image encoder
  • Each axis = mean of one group of paintings minus another (e.g. energy: Van Gogh, Turner, Goya vs Whistler, Monet)
  • Crowd = moving average (pull 0.25–0.5, stronger when you're surprising) plus discord when the room is divided
milliseconds
4 · PainterNVIDIA L4 · Google Cloud

Repaint the chapel

  • SDXL-Turbo, 4 steps, fixed seed
  • IP-Adapter in style-only mode at 1.2 takes the art-space point (palette and brushwork, no borrowed figures)
  • Canny ControlNet at 0.35 holds the chapel's outline for the first half of the steps only
1.6 s for two frames
5 · DisplayBooth screen

Your moment, then the crowd

  • Your moment: the crowd pulled 85% toward you, held for 7 s with the reading
  • Crowd after: where the room now stands, nearest paintings named
  • Visitors' words never appear on screen

How we got here

What broke, and what fixed it

broke

Repainting the last frame over and over collapsed into speckle by the third visitor.

fixed

Every frame starts fresh from the same seed. History lives in the crowd vector, not the pixels.

broke

Words as prompt directions ("calm vs agitated brushwork") barely moved the painting.

fixed

Axes defined by groups of real paintings in the image space the model actually listens to.

broke

A full-strength outline lock flattened every style into the same poster look.

fixed

Hold the outline only for the first half of the steps: layout early, style late.

broke

Anger came out bright yellow. Nothing in the library was hot, dark and violent.

fixed

Added Goya, Delacroix and Géricault. The space only reaches where its paintings live.

broke

Figure-heavy paintings leaked their crowds: anger became a brawl in front of the chapel.

fixed

Style-only injection keeps palette and drama, drops the borrowed people.

broke

Discord pinned at maximum by visitor three, since any varied night looks divided.

fixed

Discord now responds only to unusual division, and never colors your own moment.

Next

Making it feel less machine-made

Right now the person goes in as numbers and the painting comes out whole, all at once, in a smooth digital finish, with a caption that sounds like an AI wrote it. Each of those is a place to put the human back. The art history already does part of this: every style on screen comes from a painting a person made.

Recommended first

Your hand paints the change in

Now: your movement becomes four numbers and disappears.

Reveal the new painting only along the path your wrist actually traced, widened into brushstrokes over the previous painting. Each visitor leaves a visible gesture, and the crowd painting becomes literal layers of people's marks.

GPU unchanged · mask + composite on the laptop
Recommended

Let people watch it being painted

Now: a 1.4-second crossfade; the image just appears.

Lay the new frame down in brush-shaped strokes over four or five seconds, in the direction and rhythm the visitor moved. It also hides Gemini's thinking time.

display only · small
Recommended

Say it the way a docent would

Now: "radiant joy bursting with luminous affection."

Plain words that name the cause: "You spoke softly and moved slowly, so the chapel borrowed Whistler's night." The reason is more human than the adjectives.

prompt change · small
Physical materialCanvas weave and impasto lighting over the frame, or print each moment as a postcard and the final night as a canvas.
A choice for the visitorShow two takes of your moment and let the visitor keep one, or let them nudge it warmer or cooler with one gesture.
Memory with weightOlder visitors sink into the underpainting instead of averaging away, so the end of the night carries the whole night.
A human artist in the loopA local artist paints the base canvas or chooses the library, so the style space carries a person's taste, with their permission.
ImperfectionSmall seed and composition drift between visitors, like a painter who never makes the same stroke twice.
Say what is AI and what is notName the paintings and the measurements plainly. Showing the mechanism makes the result feel made, not generated.
